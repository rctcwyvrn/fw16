# 0012 — Card envelope, thickness and heatsink mounting (S2)

**Date:** 2026-10-31
**Status:** accepted 2026-10-31. The user chose the M2 SMT nuts at the BGA and KEEPOUT v1.1 with the support rubber
assumed to stay, and asked for JLCPCB's thickness options to be researched. **1.2 mm is Claude's call from that research**
(overridable). The positions below are Claude's calls too, listed for review.

## Context

`fpga/PLAN.md` S2 hands layout the board outline, mounting holes, heatsink footprint and height, and the rear-slot
position. `bay/cad/card-outline.dxf` and `card-holes.dxf` (from `bay/tools/card_outline.py`) already gave the
outline. What was missing: the heatsink's footprint and height, how it is held down, and the card thickness.
The keepout numbers (`bay/KEEPOUT.md` v1.1) put the middle zone, Z 15–43, under a 0.8 mm plate 8.65 mm above the
board. None of the card's four Ø6.2 standoff holes is within 30 mm of the BGA.

The envelope is modelled in Fusion as the `fw16-card` component of `fw16-bay-card` (project `fw16-fpga-module`).
The document also holds Framework's shell, `PCB_REF__0807` and full-laptop STEPs, all in their native frame.
The export is `bay/cad/card-envelope.step`, in component coordinates: X and Z are relative to the KiCad origin
(STEP X 7.325, Z 70.55); Y is the laptop's Y.

## Options

- **Mounting:** (a) four M2 SMT threaded nuts on the top side at the BGA; (b) arms out to the existing rear
  standoffs; (c) compression against the keyboard plate, with no fasteners.
- **Thickness:** 1.2 mm, which matches the reference and the interposer and standoff stack, or 1.6 mm, which
  costs 0.4 mm of heatsink height and leaves the interposer mating height unverified.
- **Rear strip:** KEEPOUT v1.1 with the rubber assumed to stay, v1.1 with it removed, or v1.

## Decision

- **Thickness 1.2 mm.** JLCPCB lists impedance-controlled 1.2 mm stackups at 8 layers (JLC08121H-1080,
  JLC08121H-2313, JLC081211-1080) and at 10 layers (JLC10121H-1080/-2116/-3313, JLC101211-1080). Source:
  gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups, read 2026-10-31. Confirm the chosen template in a JLCPCB quote
  before layout sets trace widths.
- **KEEPOUT v1.1 is the design basis**, with the support rubber assumed to stay: no top-side parts at Z < 15
  until calipers settle it (`bay/KEEPOUT.md`).
- **Heatsink retention: 4× M2 SMT threaded nuts** on the card's top side, screwed from above. Nothing goes below
  the card, so the Z < 23 underside no-go zone does not matter.

- **Rear slot:** none on spin 1 (0007, no rear I/O).

## Checks run (Fusion, 2026-10-31)

- The card slab extruded from `card-outline.dxf` + `card-holes.dxf` has the same bounding box as Framework's
  reference PCB body in `PCB_REF__0807` (X −57.45…61.20, Z 4.0…76.5, Y −10.65…−9.45). Its volume is within
  0.1 % of the reference (9483.7 vs 9474.2 mm³), so the DXFs are the right outline in the right frame. Both bodies have the
  same 14 through-features at the same centres (4× Ø6.2, 2× Ø2.7, 4× Ø1.5, 2× Ø1.4, Ø2.4, the 2.3 mm slot). KEEPOUT's
  "2× Ø1.0 at (−45.95, 68.0), (48.7, 68.0)" are R0.5 inside-corner fillets of the outline at the tab roots, not holes.
- No envelope body (U14, pad, heatsink, nuts) intersects any laptop, shell or reference body. Nearest solids:
  the heatsink envelope top is **0.5 mm** under `IFW70_ID_D_KBCAP` (the middle-zone ceiling is the keyboard
  plate), then 1.14 mm to the keyboard frame. U14 and the nuts are 1.5 mm from the shell frame.

## Judgment calls (review)

- **U14 centred at X 7.325**, the interposer centreline, and at Z 29.5, so the package (Z 16–43) sits inside the
  envelope's full-height band (Z 15–43). Layout
  may move it; the nuts and the heatsink envelope move with it.
- **Nut positions:** ±17.5 mm in X and ±11 mm in Z from the BGA centre. That is 4 mm outside the package's X
  edges, inside Z 15–43, and behind the Z < 15 line. The diameter is a **4.5 mm placeholder**. The board-top-to-heatsink-base distance is 3.82 mm, but that is **not
  the nut height**: a rigid 3.82 mm standoff would only meet a lid at its 3.52 mm maximum, and at the 3.31 mm nominal
  the 0.30 mm pad would float. Choose a nut shorter than 3.31 mm and let the heatsink boss plus a compliant pad or
  spring screws take up the lid tolerance (Path 1 step 5). Choose the part before layout places footprints.
- **Envelope width 40 mm in X**, which covers the nuts plus screw heads. The screw heads must be counterbored
  flush, because the envelope top has only 0.5 mm to the keyboard plate.
- **The 7343 tantalum (≈ 4 mm, top Y −5.45) cannot go under the heatsink**, whose base is at −5.63. Place it
  outside the envelope's X span, or in the Z 15–43 band beside it.

## Consequences

- Layout can start from `card-outline.dxf`, `card-holes.dxf` and the nut and U14 table above.
- Path 1 step 5 (heatsink design) designs within this envelope. Still open: fins along X in the ~2.4 mm air band
  versus a spreader with a pad up to the keyboard plate, and the material. Step 4's dummy card prints this STEP.
- **Nut part chosen 2026-10-31: Würth WA-SMSI M2, L 3.0 ± 0.1 mm (9774030243R)**, OD 4.35 mm, tin over steel, 0.2 N·m
  max. Land pattern per its datasheet (rev 001.003): Ø5.3 SMD pad around a **Ø3.0 non-plated through hole**, stencil
  annulus Ø5.2/Ø3.1. Footprint `fw16-fpga-module:Wurth_WA-SMSI_M2_9774030243R`; symbols H1–H4 on the `fpga` sheet,
  pad on GND. The 3.0 mm nut sits 0.31 mm below the lid's 3.31 mm nominal top, so the heatsink ears step down by
  that plus the pad's compression (Path 1 step 5). The NPTH goes through the board, so the M2 screw length must keep
  its tip inside the nut: at nuts 1/2 (Z 18.5) the floor gap below is only 0.30 mm.

# 0006 — Card FPGA: XCAU15P-2FFVB676E

**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Status:** accepted (subject to quote; see Consequences)

## Context

The card needs a PCIe Gen4 x4 endpoint inside the bay's ~19 W budget, a Vivado/XDMA flow that keeps the
Hardcaml work continuous with the AUBoard 15P, and a package a controlled-impedance hobby fab can assemble.
Per AMD's UltraScale+ data sheet and product selection guide, the AU15P's PCIe Gen4 capability exists **only
in the FFVB676 package**; SBVB484 and UBVA368 are limited to Gen3 and their GTH line rate is capped at
12.5 Gb/s (16.3 Gb/s in FFVB676). The AUBoard 15P uses XCAU15P-2FFVB676E.

## Options

| Part | Body / pitch | GTH | PCIe | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| XCAU15P-2FFVB676E | 27 × 27 mm, 1.0 mm | 12 | Gen4 | Same part as the AUBoard; easiest pitch to route and assemble |
| XCAU15P-2SBVB484E | 19 × 19 mm, 0.8 mm | 12 | Gen3 | Smaller card, permanent Gen3 ceiling, pinout diverges from the AUBoard |
| XCAU15P-xUBVA368x | 11.5 × 9.5 mm, 0.5 mm | 8 | Gen3 | HDI stackup needed; rejected |
| Efinix Ti375 (N484) | 18 × 18 mm, 0.8 mm | — | Gen4 x4 hard block | Fallback only: new toolchain, no Hardcaml→Efinity path proven |

## Decision

**XCAU15P-2FFVB676E.** Speed grade -2 as on the AUBoard (whether -1 also meets Gen4 is not stated in the
sources found; check DS931/PG213 before buying a -1 to save money). Temperature grade E (0–100 °C Tj); the
laptop's own operating range is narrower than industrial. Vivado ML Standard (free) lists XCAU15P (UG973).

## Consequences

- The card's PCIe lanes are designed for Gen4 from spin 1; the hard block is still configured with Gen3
  fallback so a marginal spin 1 remains useful.
- 27 × 27 mm body: confirm against `bay/KEEPOUT.md` before schematic; expected to fit the open middle area.
- In the SOM-carrier branch of [0007](0007-card-architecture.md) the part arrives on the module as
  `XCAU15P-2FFVB676I`; the package conclusion here is unchanged. The chip-down branch buys `-2FFVB676E`.
- If the quote exceeds ~$300/unit or the part is allocated, revisit: SBVB484 (if available cheaper) accepts
  Gen3 forever; Ti375 changes the toolchain. Either reopens this record.
